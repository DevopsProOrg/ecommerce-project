Catalog guidance

A few org-style rules to keep the marketplace clean as you scale.

Use categories broadly
Keep `Fashion`, `Accessories`, `Lifestyle`, and future categories broad, then use subcategory for the exact product family.
Keep SKU stable
SKU should stay unique even if the title changes. This helps later when we add orders and reporting.
Write highlights for cards
Short comma-separated highlights become bullets in the shopper UI and make each item feel more realistic.
Use featured items carefully
Featured products are shown more prominently, so use them for hero launches or high-conversion products.
